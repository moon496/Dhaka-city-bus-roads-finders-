About our project

Making Dhaka Bus Travel Easier to Explore

A student software engineering project to make local bus route information simpler to browse.

🚌
OUR PURPOSE

Clearer route information for everyday journeys

Dhaka Bus Route Finder helps commuters explore the bus services and stops included in the project's route list. The goal is to make it easier to look up a service, understand its listed stop order, and plan where to continue a journey.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Explore the project

Use the pages to browse route information in a few simple ways.

🔎

Find a bus

Look up bus services and route options for stops in the available dataset.

📍

Browse nearby stops

Search for a stop by name and see which listed routes pass through it.

🗺

Explore route order

Follow the stops of a selected service in the order recorded by the project.

DATA & LIMITATIONS

Built around the information we have

Route details come from the project's bus dataset and may not cover every service or reflect current road operations. The supplied records do not include stop coordinates, so nearby search uses stop names and the route view is schematic—not a live geographic map. Please verify service details locally before travelling.
